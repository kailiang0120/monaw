The Windows lxml extension modules (lxml/*.pyd) are statically linked with:

- libxml2 and libxslt, under the MIT-style licenses in the "libxml2 and libxslt"
  section of this file.
- zlib, under the zlib license (see the zlib section of the Python runtime).
- GNU libiconv, copyright Free Software Foundation, Inc., under the GNU Lesser
  General Public License version 2.1 or later. The LGPL 2.1 text is included with
  the tld package in this file.

The libraries are built by https://github.com/lxml/libxml2-win-binaries; libiconv
source is at https://ftp.gnu.org/pub/gnu/libiconv/ and lxml source at
https://github.com/lxml/lxml. The lxml modules are separate files under
resources/backend/_internal/lxml and can be replaced with a build of lxml linked
against a modified libiconv.
